World Magnetic Model 2025 (WMM2025)

Coefficients produced jointly by the US National Centers for Environmental
Information (NCEI) and the British Geological Survey (BGS), and distributed by
NCEI on behalf of the US National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency and by BGS on
behalf of the UK Defence Geographic Centre.

https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/world-magnetic-model

The model is released for public use. This application embeds the WMM2025
Gauss coefficients, valid from 2025.0 to 2030.0, and evaluates them to obtain
the magnetic declination used to correct the device compass to true north.
Outside that window the application reports the value as out of date rather
than using it silently.

The World Magnetic Model and the associated software and documentation are in
the public domain and are not subject to copyright protection.
