Constellation figures

The constellation line figures are taken from the d3-celestial project by
Olaf Frohn, file data/constellations.lines.json, and are redistributed under
the BSD 3-Clause License reproduced alongside this notice.
https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial

The underlying line geometry derives from the constellation charts produced
by the International Astronomical Union in collaboration with Sky & Telescope
magazine (Roger Sinnott and Rick Fienberg), released under the Creative
Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

These are figure lines, not official IAU constellation boundaries. The IAU
standardises the boundaries between constellations; the stick figures joining
the stars are a drawing convention, not a standard.
